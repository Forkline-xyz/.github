# Forkline

**Trade tokenized public equities on-chain. Non-custodial. Fully verifiable.**

[forkline.xyz](https://forkline.xyz)

</div>

---

Forkline is a self-custody trading terminal for tokenized public equities — trade names like AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA, GOOGL, AMZN, META, SPY, and QQQ as on-chain Position NFTs, held directly in your own wallet. No broker custody, no IOUs — every position you hold is verifiable on-chain.

Holders of the **`$FORKE`** token earn a live, on-chain revenue share of protocol trading fees. A treasury wallet funds a new reward round roughly every 24 hours, splitting a fixed daily `$FORKE` allocation across every holder proportional to their live balance — fully transparent, with every funding round and every claim a real, verifiable on-chain event.
